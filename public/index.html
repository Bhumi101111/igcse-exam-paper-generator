IGCSE Paper Generator

Builds Physics · Chemistry · Mathematics papers from a built-in question bank (PMT/CAIE style).

Papers are built from the built-in question bank by default — just pick chapters and generate. To mix in real past-paper questions, upload PDFs or paste direct PDF links and they'll be combined with the bank. (Reference: pastpapers.co/caie/igcse — its index can't be crawled by the server, so download PDFs and upload them.)
